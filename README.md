
## Hi, I'm EszakiGabor (Seza44) (seza.system)👋

I have been actively working with origami since 2008. 


My creative work is rooted in geometry, complex modular structures, kusudama and original model design. Over the years, I have designed, developed and documented numerous original origami models. My work includes both the creation of physical models and the development of folding diagrams that document their structure and construction.

Among my original designs are Kusudama Sandy Hook, Kusudama Stegosaurus, Kusudama Stegosaurus 2, Crane Kusudama, Kusudama Hope Star, and Kusudama Kalavalecz. Folding diagrams and documentation for several of these models are preserved as part of my own work.

I have been an active part of the Hungarian origami community for many years, including my connection with the Hungarian Origami Society (Magyar Origami Kör). My models and folding instructions have appeared in Hungarian origami publications, including the Ori-Magi magazine. My works have also been presented in Hungarian origami exhibitions and travelling exhibitions. Among these was “The Wonderful World of Origami” (Az origami csodálatos világa), a travelling exhibition whose documentation also records my participation and works.

One of the important international chapters of my origami work is connected to Kusudama Sandy Hook. I designed this original modular kusudama in connection with the international origami initiative following the Sandy Hook tragedy. The project was associated with Folding for Good and the international community initiative connected with Origami Salami. The resulting origami works were exhibited in Pittsburgh, USA, including a presentation connected with the Carnegie Science Center. This project became an important part of my international origami experience and connected my work with an international community of origami artists and creators.

Origami has remained an important foundation of my creative thinking. For me, it is not only the folding of paper, but also a way of working with geometry, structure, repetition, modularity, proportion and visual systems. Designing an origami model means developing a structure from an idea and understanding how individual elements can become a coherent whole.

## Digital Art and SEZA.SYSTEM

Alongside my long-term work in origami, I have developed a separate digital creative field called SEZA.SYSTEM.

SEZA.SYSTEM is an independent digital art and visual world built around geometry, visual systems, digital creation and AI-assisted creative work. It grows from the same creative background of structural and geometric thinking, while existing as its own distinct artistic field.

In SEZA.SYSTEM, I work together with AI collaborators.

AI is not the artist behind the project and it does not replace my role as the creator. It is part of my creative team. I develop the concepts, visual direction, structures, ideas and overall artistic system, while AI collaborators participate in the development and realization of the digital work.

The relationship between my origami work and SEZA.SYSTEM is therefore not that they are the same project. They are two distinct creative worlds connected through me as the artist: one rooted in physical paper, geometry and model design, and the other developed through digital art, visual systems and collaboration with AI.
